# Audience: First Commit

## Who they are

First and second year IT and computer science students. Self taught beginners learning Python or web development. People switching careers into tech who feel lost in jargon. Source: ../../BRAND.md, read 2026-11-05.

## What they already know

Very little: they are writing their first lines of code. They can install an editor and open a terminal, and most of the vocabulary around Git, the command line and the web is still jargon to them.

## What they are trying to do

Get short, correct, practical tips they can try in five minutes, explained without jargon. Stop the tools getting in their way, save their work safely, and understand what happens behind the screen.

## Where they already are

Instagram, where First Commit posts: https://www.instagram.com/firstcommit.tips/ (linked from the website, read 2026-11-05). Wider audience places were not researched on this install. soc-material-sweep fills them on its next run.

## Working days and hours

Students in Riga, Latvia. Brand cadence is Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, plus Stories on post days (../../BRAND.md). Posting hours not yet observed: no published post has been read.
